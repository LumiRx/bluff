Support

We answer everything at info@webluff.com

My record disappeared

BLUFF keeps your record on your own device, not on a server. Clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or moving to a different device or browser starts you fresh. In the installed app your record persists until you delete the app or use Reset career.

I cannot hear anything

On iPhone, the ringer switch mutes web audio. Flip it off silent. There is also a speaker button at the top of the home screen and separate sound and music switches in your profile.

Is there any money in this?

None. Nothing is for sale, nothing pays out, and there is no way to spend money on BLUFF even if you wanted to. The top three on the daily board take 3,000, 1,500 and 750 stars, and stars buy looks inside the game and nothing else. The whole arrangement is in the house rules.

I placed in the top three. What now?

The stars are already in your profile — they land as soon as the day's board settles at midnight UTC, with nothing to claim and nobody to write to. Spend them on a new felt, a new set of tiles, or save them.

How do I play with a friend?

Add their handle in Friends, then tap their name. You both get the same table for that day without sending each other anything — the code is derived from your two handles. There is also a table code and a QR you can share directly.

Can I buy my way to the top of the board?

No, and this is deliberate. Stars buy looks. They cannot be bought with money, cannot be spent on the daily, cannot improve a placing, and are not part of the ranking. Power-ups are earned at the table and are never sold.

I want my data deleted

Reset career in your profile erases everything held on your device. The board keeps only the handle and the six decisions of each finished daily, so write to info@webluff.com with your handle if you want those taken off it, and we will handle the rest within 30 days.

Something is broken

Write to info@webluff.com and tell us your handle, your device, and what you were doing. Screenshots help enormously.
