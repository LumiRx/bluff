House rules

The BLUFF Daily · last updated 8 August 2026
There is no money in BLUFF. Nothing is for sale, nothing pays out, and stars never leave the game. What follows is how the daily works and what is expected of you at the table.

1. The daily

Every player in the world is dealt the identical six hands each day — the same words, the same clues, the same prices. Hands are four, five or six letters. A daily runs until midnight UTC; standings close at 23:59:59 UTC and six new hands are dealt for the new day. It is free, it takes about five minutes, and it costs no stars — there is nothing you could buy that would move you up its board.

2. What the board ranks

The board ranks decisions. It scores what you cracked, what you held as setter, and which calls you were right to refuse. Everyone faced the same six problems in the same order, so a standing is a comparison of choices and nothing else.

3. Stars

Stars are a score. They buy cosmetic looks — felts, tiles, card backs — and nothing else. They cannot be bought with money, cannot be cashed out, cannot be traded, and cannot improve a placing on any board. We may adjust a balance to correct a bug or to reverse abuse.

4. Power-ups

Power-ups are earned in play. They are never sold, so nobody arrives at a table holding more than you could have earned yourself.

5. One run a day

One run per device per day, and the first run is the one that stands. Finish it or bust it — either way, that was your day. Reinstalling, clearing storage or opening a second browser does not buy a second attempt at the board.

6. The score comes out of the play

A finished run is re-scored on the server from the decisions that produced it. Nothing accepts a score as an input: the only thing your device can send is the play, and the number comes back out of it. A run that does not re-score to what it claims does not stand.

7. Handles

Your handle appears on a public board, so choose one you would be happy to see there. Handles that impersonate someone, advertise, or are abusive are refused, and any that get through are removed.

8. Automated play

Bots, scripts, solvers driving the board, and any tampering with the daily deal void the run. Do it repeatedly and you lose access to the board.

9. When a day goes wrong

If a technical fault or tampering compromises a day, we may void that day's standings, and we will say so rather than quietly restate the numbers. If something looks wrong to you, write to support@webluff.com with your handle and the date.

10. Who runs this

5arz Services LLC · support@webluff.com. Everything sent there is read.
