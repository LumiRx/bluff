BLUFF is a daily word game. You are dealt a hand of four, five or six letters and decide whether to commit to a guess or fold.

THERE IS NO MONEY IN THIS APP. No real-money wagering, no prizes, no cash-out, and no in-app purchases (none are configured on this record). "Stars" are a score. They can be spent only on cosmetic table looks and cannot be converted into anything.

NO ADVERTISING. This build contains no advertising SDK, no advertising identifier and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. The privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false with an empty tracking-domain list.

THE OTHER FIVE SEATS ARE BOTS. There are no other human players, live or asynchronous, anywhere in the app.

NO CHAT AND NO USER-GENERATED CONTENT. The bots at the table show short speech bubbles ("Called it.", "No chance."). Every one of those lines is a fixed string compiled into the app binary; there is no server, no moderation queue and no other user behind them. The player can answer with one of five fixed reactions - CALLED IT, NO CHANCE, RESPECT, OUCH, BLUFF! - chosen from a bar of five buttons. There is no free-text field anywhere on the table, nothing a player types is ever shown to anyone else, and nothing is transmitted between users. The whole feature can be switched off in Settings.

NEW IN 1.1.5 - AN OPTIONAL X (TWITTER) CONNECTION. The profile screen offers "CONNECT X". It is entirely optional, it gates nothing, and the app is complete without it.

  What it does: the player is taken to X's own sign-in page in the system browser (OAuth 2.0 with PKCE; scopes users.read and tweet.read - both read-only). On return, the app has one thing: the player's public @handle, shown on their own profile so a friend recognises them. The access token is read once on our server to fetch that handle and is then discarded - it is never stored, never returned to the app, and never written to the device. No posts are read, no follower list is read, nothing is written to the player's X account, and nothing is posted on their behalf, ever.

  How to remove it: the same row becomes "DISCONNECT", which clears the handle from the device immediately. Settings > Reset Career also clears it. Because we store no token, disconnecting leaves nothing behind on our side.

  Sharing is separate and needs no connection. "POST TO X" (on the share strip and on the profile card) opens X's public web intent with the text already written out. The player reads it and presses Post themselves. The app has no ability to post without that press. This is a plain https link to x.com - no SDK, no API credential, no login required.

NO ACCOUNT IS REQUIRED and none is created unless the player asks for one. The app is fully playable from first launch with no sign-up, so there are no demo credentials to supply. Three optional things exist and none of them gates any gameplay: (1) Settings > KEEP YOUR RECORD offers a phone sign-in, only so a record can move to a new phone; (2) an optional single email line, used to say when a new edition deals and for nothing else; (3) the X connection described above. All three are opt-in and all three can be removed in the app.

ACCOUNT DELETION / DATA ERASURE: Settings > Reset Career erases everything the app holds on the device, immediately and permanently - including any connected X handle. If the optional phone sign-in was used, Settings > DELETE ACCOUNT erases the server-side record and releases the handle.

The only text a player can enter is a display handle, checked against a profanity list (with leetspeak folding) before it can appear on the public board.

The daily board is a leaderboard on which players are ranked against one another. It has no entry fee and no prize of any kind.

The app records anonymous first-party usage counts only, to its own server. No third-party SDKs, no cookies, no device identifiers and no cross-app tracking.

All audio is synthesised at runtime. The answer list is 3,576 curated words, each shown at the end of a hand with its pronunciation and meaning; guesses are validated against a standard word list. No third-party content is included.
