Your profile is a place now, not a list of switches. A crest, your tier, and THE CABINET — the shelf where your best bluff, your biggest hand and your longest run sit out where you can see them. Every toggle moved to Settings, where it belongs.

CONNECTIONS puts your friends on your profile as faces you can tap to call someone out.

SHARE MY CARD makes a card worth sending: your handle, your tier, and the word you held that nobody cracked. It deals out line by line.

And you can connect X if you want to — just so a friend sees who you are. It reads your handle and nothing else, it never posts for you, and the game does not need it.
